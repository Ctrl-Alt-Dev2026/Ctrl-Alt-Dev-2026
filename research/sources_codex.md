# SafeIași: verified data sources and acquisition paths

Research date: **18 October 2026, Europe/Bucharest**. Researcher: Codex. Scope: the six priority checks and both tracks in `DATA_SOURCES_RESEARCH.md`. This is research only; no requests were sent to institutions and this research changed no application code.

**Usable official sources exist today, but this investigation did not establish an openly downloadable, complete register of individual Iași crashes.** The strongest immediate improvement is to combine official ISU incident bulletins with the PMUD street baseline and INS county totals. The business path is an anonymised export from IPJ, complemented by the traffic-management event log and traffic counts. Published aggregates must remain aggregates; they cannot be turned into individual events or precise crash points.

## 1. Top five, ranked by practical value

Scores are in this order: **G/C/H/F/P/A/L/E** = granularity, coverage, history, freshness, provenance, access, commercial licence, ease of use. Each score is 1–5; higher is better. Scores describe the material actually verified, not the hoped-for contents of a future export. Unknown reuse rights score 1–2, rather than being assumed open. Rankings also consider relevance to SafeIași, so they are not a sum-of-scores league table.

### 1. PMUD final document: a real municipal street baseline

The [PMUD labelled FINAL](https://vision4iasi.ro/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/PMUD-Iasi-FINAL.pdf) returned **HTTP 200, PDF**. Its extracted text is identical to the [ZMI copy labelled Reactualizare 2022](https://zmi.ro/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/PMUD-Iasi-Reactualizare-2022.pdf). Table 2.11 on printed/PDF page 73 lists twelve streets for 2020 and 2021, with IPJ Iași credited as the source. A real row is **Nicolina: 40 in 2020; 43 in 2021**. Figure 2.55 compares the listed streets in a bar chart; the adjoining section contains historical crash/consequence charts. These are street totals, without event coordinates or dates. **Scores: 3/3/5/1/5/4/2/4. Verdict: use now**, as a dated official baseline. Next: request the underlying IPJ export and the latest adopted PMUD/HCL and annexes. A file named FINAL is not proof that no later adopted revision exists. [Saved text](samples/codex/pmud_2023_final_extracted.txt).

### 2. ISU Iași incident PDFs: the strongest newly verified event source

The [2026 press-news index](https://isuis.igsu.ro/comunicate-de-presa/stiri-de-presa-2026-175) and the sampled PDFs returned **HTTP 200, HTML/PDF**. [Bulletin 561](https://isuis.igsu.ro/resources/ae87d9e9-2f76-4f04-88aa-2f2d5fe1c640.pdf) describes an intervention on **13 May 2026 at 10:25, șoseaua Bucium**, involving two cars, two conscious, non-trapped casualties and one hospital transport; publication is **15 May**, not the event date. [Bulletin 574](https://isuis.igsu.ro/resources/c8d6ff3d-c936-4b84-a859-c01b2d7df181.pdf) gives another dated intervention on Nicolina. The index contains 144 titles mentioning `rutier`; this is a candidate-document count, **not a count of unique crashes**, and includes an exercise. Coverage is emergency-service interventions selected for publication. **Scores: 3/2/1/4/5/4/2/4. Verdict: use now.** Next: extract a small reviewed batch, retain source/event/publication dates separately, exclude exercises and deduplicate against press/IPJ records. [Sample PDF](samples/codex/isu_bucium.pdf), [text](samples/codex/isu_bucium_extracted.txt).

### 3. INS TEMPO: working JSON and CSV, with county totals through 2025

The official endpoints [TRN104F](http://statistici.insse.ro:8077/tempo-ins/matrix/TRN104F) and [TRN105B](http://statistici.insse.ro:8077/tempo-ins/matrix/TRN105B) returned **HTTP 200, JSON**. POST selections and CSV export through [`/pivot`](http://statistici.insse.ro:8077/tempo-ins/pivot) also worked. Verified Iași county rows: **1,389 injury crashes in 2024; 1,462 in 2025; 56/58 deaths and 1,695/1,799 injured people respectively**. These are annual county aggregates; the geography dimension has counties/regions, not municipalities, streets or individual crashes. Metadata was updated 22 June 2026. **Scores: 1/3/5/2/5/5/2/5. Verdict: use now**, for denominators, coverage checks and business reports; request clarification of INS commercial bulk redistribution terms. Next: obtain the 2021–2025 county series, retain definitions and revisions, and compare compatible scopes. Do not interpret 1,462 as damage-only-inclusive total crashes. [Crash CSV](samples/codex/tempo_county_crashes_export.csv), [casualty CSV](samples/codex/tempo_casualties_export.csv).

### 4. Poliția Locală: documented camera-observed crashes and a concrete export request

The [2025 activity report](https://politialocala-iasi.ro/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/RAPORT-DE-EVALUARE-2025.pdf) returned **HTTP 200, PDF**. Printed page 26 reports camera-observed crashes monitored in C.M.T.: **127 with victims and 432 without victims in 2025**, versus 140 and 500 in 2024. This establishes a separate operational source covering some property-damage incidents too. It is a **camera-observed subset**, not a citywide crash census, and only annual totals are published in the sampled document. **Scores: 1/2/2/2/5/4/2/4. Verdict: use for the business.** Use the published aggregates immediately, but request the underlying anonymised event log, camera coverage and collection definitions before mapping events. A tailored request and a verified institutional email are below. [Saved report](samples/codex/local_police_pdf.pdf), [extracted text](samples/codex/local_police_pdf_extracted.txt).

### 5. SEAP through data.gov.ro: actual buyers and completed purchases

The [2024 official procurement dataset](https://data.gov.ro/dataset/achizitii-publice-2024) exposes working CKAN DataStore JSON for some resources, under **CC BY 4.0**. Three completed road-related service purchases were verified directly: **Ardud, road-safety audit, 17,340 RON, 22 January 2024; Bistrița, traffic study for traffic-management extension, 203,054.40 RON, completed 18 December 2024; Vaslui, corridor traffic study, 2,500 RON, completed 23 December 2024**. These prove procurement activity, not a price for our proposed report. **Scores: 1/1/1/2/5/5/5/5. Verdict: use now.** Next: search Iași buyers and adjacent engineering firms by CPV and object, then read specifications to compare scope. Quarterly procurement data needs no login where DataStore is enabled. [Ardud API sample](samples/codex/market_datastore_ardud.json), [traffic-study API sample](samples/codex/market_datastore_traffic.json).

### What can improve the demo today

1. Add manually checked ISU events and source links, with street-level uncertainty retained. The two sampled bulletins support intervention times, not necessarily the exact collision times.
2. Display the PMUD table as a **2020–2021 street baseline**. Do not combine its counts with individual events, place all street crashes at one intersection, or label these totals as current risk.
3. Use INS totals to explain sample incompleteness. For example, the 2025 county total is 1,462 injury crashes, while the demo concerns a selected local sample; neither the geography nor capture mechanism is identical.
4. Expand press discovery using the newly verified WordPress APIs in the media table. A query match is not an accident: arts events, aviation, prevention stories and national news appeared in real results.
5. Add transit-stop context from the verified Iași GTFS feed after checking reuse attribution/terms. Stop locations and route schedules describe infrastructure and service, not pedestrian flows or vehicle speeds.

### What makes a paid report defensible

Prioritise IPJ anonymised crash records, C.M.T. operational events and CESTRIN/municipal traffic counts. Publish the reporting period, geography, exclusions, geocoding uncertainty and source lineage for every result. A prioritisation score from incomplete reporting is not a probability of a future crash or evidence that an intervention will save a quantified number of lives.

INS definitions explicitly exclude damage-only crashes from the injury-crash tables. Deaths are defined within 30 days of the accident; injuries combine serious and slight injuries. The sampled 2025 INS injury count is 1,799. A press summary encountered during discovery gave a different combined injury count; it was not substituted into the official series. Reconcile extraction dates, definitions and revisions with IPJ rather than silently mixing figures.

## 3. Full source register

The tables below group versions/endpoints belonging to one source. The endpoint audit at the end records **every direct HTTP attempt**, including failed guesses and redirects. A successful HTTP response can be an error page, a JavaScript shell or HTML returned at a supposed RSS URL. Such responses are identified explicitly. `UNVERIFIED` means the requested capability or data was not established; it does not mean the institution has no such data.

For context sources, G measures the location precision of that context, not evidence that a crash occurred there. Coverage scores remain conservative where a source is a subset, a catalogue, or outside Iași. `use now` means usable within the stated purpose and rights, not blanket permission to resell the source database.

### Privacy and interpretation

The [GDPR text](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj/eng), particularly minimisation and restrictions around health/criminal-offence data, matters even for public court/police records. Public availability and replacing names with initials do not automatically make a record anonymous. A precise timestamp/location can allow linkage to identifiable victims. Request anonymous or appropriately generalised records; agree publication granularity and retention with the institution/legal adviser. Do not build individual driver/victim profiles. Court SOAP response bodies containing parties were discarded; the sample log retains only limited case metadata.

The three FOI drafts ask for existing records and partial-access fallbacks rather than asserting an entitlement to every microdata field. Permission to disclose information and permission to commercially redistribute an entire database are related but separate questions. No dataset-specific commercial grant is claimed where it was not verified.

Weather/light should be separate derived fields with a method/source label. Unknown event time remains null. Astronomical night requires an explicit timezone and light threshold; modelled rain at a grid cell is context rather than proof of the conditions on that road. Speeds from transit vehicles or congestion indices are not all-vehicle speed distributions; stop locations and Strava users are not unbiased pedestrian counts.

### International request-format template

UK and France samples show a realistic request structure: one accident identifier linked to separate event, vehicle and casualty tables, with date/time, road/location, severity, participants, weather/light/surface codes and a data dictionary. Germany's metadata adds explicit coordinate systems and location-quality checks. Ask for these fields **where they already exist**, without requiring a Romanian institution to reproduce another country's schema or disclose protected data. The verified comparison supports “other countries publish event-level injury-crash data”; it does **not** establish the universal claim that Romania publishes none.
